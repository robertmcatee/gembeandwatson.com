About the Estate

Gembe & Watson was established in 2009 to give a family’s interests a permanent home — one that would outlast any single venture, and any single lifetime.

The estate holds those interests separately from the family’s other affairs, and administers them with a long view: preserve what matters, improve what can be improved, and pass both on in good order.

Its work is public, charitable, and private in turn — giving in the community, and support for the family’s commitments at home and in Texas. That work is undertaken quietly, and it is not publicized.

The Emblem

The estate’s emblem is a serpent entwined with six fretted arrows, within a ring bearing the motto Vis Nescia Vinci — “a force that knows not how to be conquered.”

The device is taken from an emblem-book engraving: the serpent wound through arrows bound together — strength in what is joined, and a resolve that does not yield. It is the mark of the estate, borne on its site and its papers.

Hostibus adversis concors VIS NESCIA VINCI,
Pugnat coniunctis viribus, ac animis.

“Against opposing enemies, a united force that knows no defeat fights with joined strengths and minds.”

Invincibility is there where Order, Strength, and Union are.
